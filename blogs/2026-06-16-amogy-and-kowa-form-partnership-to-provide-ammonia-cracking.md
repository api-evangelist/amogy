NEW YORK AND TOKYO, JAPAN — June 16, 2026 — Amogy , a provider of mature, scalable, and efficient ammonia-to-hydrogen and ammonia-to-power solutions, and KOWA Company, Ltd. (KOWA) today announced a partnership to bring ammonia cracking-based hydrogen supply solutions in Japan, following the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the two companies in April 2026.
